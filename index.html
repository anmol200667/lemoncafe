Lemon Jelly Café

Lemon Jelly Café is an independent café in Dublin City Centre, just a short walk from Temple Bar. Open daily from 6:30am, we serve all-day breakfast, brunch and lunch, including Full Irish Breakfasts, speciality coffee, matcha, fresh smoothies, açaí bowls, freshly made juices, gluten-free options, sweet and savoury crêpes and fluffy omelettes. Whether you're a local, commuter, tourist or visiting Temple Bar, you'll find fresh food, friendly service and a relaxed atmosphere with comfortable indoor and outdoor seating. With over 7,500 Google reviews, Lemon Jelly Café has become one of Dublin's favourite destinations for breakfast and brunch.

At Lemon Jelly Café, food is served from two menus. Breakfast is available all day, including our much loved traditional Irish breakfast, while our all day lunch menu features savoury and sweet crepes, salads, omelettes and ciabattas.

Our friendly service and upbeat atmosphere make it just as easy to pop in for a quick bite as it is to settle in for a relaxed afternoon. Whether you're watching the city go by outside or catching up with friends, you'll always feel welcome here.
